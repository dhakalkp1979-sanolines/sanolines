Text Reverser

Instantly reverse text characters, words, or entire lines with a single click.

What is a Text Reverser?

A Text Reverser is a handy utility designed to flip text strings backward. Whether you want to reverse every individual character, invert the order of words, or swap lines of a multi-line code block or poem, this tool handles transformations instantly.

Modes of Text Reversal

  • Reverse Characters: Flips every letter and symbol backward (using safe Unicode string splitting for emojis and special characters).
  • Reverse Words: Keeps individual words intact while reversing their overall sequence in the sentence.
  • Reverse Lines: Inverts the order of multi-line text entries, moving the last line to the top.

Fast, Local, and Private Processing

This tool runs entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your notes, text snippets, and data remain entirely private on your device without ever being uploaded or stored externally.
