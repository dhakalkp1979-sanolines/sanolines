U.S.–Iran tensions rise around the Strait of Hormuz

Renewed tensions involving the United States and Iran have increased concerns about regional security and the potential impact on global energy markets.

The Strait of Hormuz remains an important international shipping route, with developments in the region being closely watched by governments, businesses and financial markets.

Any prolonged disruption in the area could affect energy supplies, transportation costs and international markets.
