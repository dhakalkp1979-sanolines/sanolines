Case Converter

Easily convert text to uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, and more.

What is a Case Converter?

A Case Converter is a quick text-formatting utility that allows you to transform paragraphs, strings, titles, or code snippets instantly between different letter-casing styles. Instead of manually retyping text, you can change cases with a single click.

Available Casing Formats

  • UPPERCASE: Capitalizes every single letter in your text block, ideal for headings or emphasis.
  • lowercase: Converts all characters into lowercase letters.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of every major word, perfect for article titles and headers.
  • Sentence case: Automatically capitalizes the first letter of each sentence following standard punctuation.
  • aLtErNaTe cAsE: Alternates letter formatting between lowercase and uppercase for stylized or playful text.

Private and Instant Browser Processing

This tool runs entirely on the client side using built-in JavaScript. Your text inputs remain completely secure and private within your device, ensuring nothing is ever uploaded to external servers.
