How to Calculate Flooring and Tile Requirements

Determining the correct amount of flooring or tile material for a room ensures you have enough coverage while minimizing costly excess. By multiplying the room length by the width, you obtain the base square footage required for installation.

Why Include a Waste Allowance?

  • Cuts and Trims: Perimeter cuts, corners, and irregular angles inevitably result in material scrap.
  • Breakage Buffer: Ceramic tiles, planks, or stone tiles can occasionally break during handling or cutting.
  • Recommended Buffer: A standard 10% waste allowance is typically recommended for standard layouts, while diagonal patterns may require 15% or more.

Tips for Accurate Measurements

Always measure from the longest and widest points of the room, and account for closets, alcoves, or entryways separately to prevent any shortage of materials during your home improvement project.
