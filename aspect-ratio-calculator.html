What is an Aspect Ratio?

An aspect ratio is the proportional relationship between an image or video's width and its height, typically expressed as two numbers separated by a colon (such as 16:9 or 4:3). Maintaining the correct ratio ensures that media scales properly without stretching, squishing, or distorting visuals.

How to Use the Aspect Ratio Calculator

  • Enter your starting dimensions in the Original Width and Original Height fields to determine the base ratio.
  • Modify either the New Width or New Height to automatically calculate the matching proportional dimension.
  • View the simplified aspect ratio instantly in the results panel.

Common Screen and Media Ratios

Standard widescreen monitors and high-definition video utilize 16:9, traditional television formats use 4:3, cinema screens often use 21:9 or 2.39:1, and vertical smartphone content relies heavily on 9:16 proportions.
