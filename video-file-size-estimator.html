What is a Video File Size Estimator?

A Video File Size Estimator is a handy tool that projects how much disk space or storage a recorded or rendered video will consume based on its total duration and combined data rates. It helps creators manage storage limits and meet hosting upload criteria.

How to Use the Tool

  • Duration: Enter the length of the video in minutes and seconds.
  • Video Bitrate: Input your project's target video compression rate in kilobits per second (kbps).
  • Audio Bitrate: Select the audio track quality setting to include its impact on the final size.

Factors Affecting Video Storage

Final file size is directly proportional to length and bitrate. Higher resolutions, faster frame rates, and multi-channel audio tracks require higher bitrates, resulting in larger files that demand efficient compression codecs like H.264, HEVC (H.265), or AV1.
