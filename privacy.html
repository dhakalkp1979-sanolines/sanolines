Privacy Policy

Information about how Sanolines handles privacy, website usage and personal information.

Privacy matters: Sanolines aims to respect your privacy and provide clear information about how this website may handle information when you use our services.

1. About Sanolines

Sanolines is an independent global information and services platform designed to help users find useful information, official resources, practical services and online tools.

Sanolines may provide links to external websites, including government authorities, public organisations, companies and other services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, limited technical or usage information may be collected automatically by hosting, security, analytics or other website services.

This information may include:

  • IP address or approximate location
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of website access
  • Referring website information
  • Technical information needed for security and operation

3. Information You Provide

Sanolines may receive personal information only when you voluntarily provide it through a feature or service that requests such information.

Examples could include information submitted through contact forms, communications or other future website features.

Sanolines does not require users to provide personal information simply to browse general information on the website.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used where necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain the website
  • Improve website performance and usability
  • Protect the website against abuse or security threats
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Understand general website usage
  • Develop useful services and tools

5. Cookies

Sanolines may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to support website functionality, security, analytics or other services.

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on a device by a website or service.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate information or consent mechanisms will be provided before using non-essential cookies.

6. Analytics

Sanolines may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content, performance and user experience.

If analytics services are introduced, the relevant services may process technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

7. External Links

Sanolines may link to external websites and services.

When you leave Sanolines and visit another website, that website may collect and process information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Sanolines does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

8. Third-Party Services

Sanolines may use third-party providers for services such as hosting, security, analytics, advertising, affiliate services or website functionality.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Affiliate Links

Sanolines may participate in affiliate programmes in the future.

When a user clicks an affiliate link and completes a qualifying action on an external website, Sanolines may receive a commission at no additional cost to the user.

Affiliate partners may use cookies or other tracking technologies to identify referrals.

10. Data Security

Sanolines aims to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Personal information will not be retained for longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by applicable law.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include rights to:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally applicable
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

13. European Users

Sanolines may be accessed by users in the European Economic Area and other countries.

Where applicable, personal data may be processed in accordance with relevant data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Where required, users will be provided with appropriate information about the legal basis for processing and applicable privacy rights.

14. Children's Privacy

Sanolines is intended to provide general information and services for a broad audience.

Sanolines does not knowingly request personal information from children where such collection would be prohibited by applicable law.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sanolines may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the website, services or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

16. Contact

If you have a privacy-related question or request, please use the contact method provided on the Sanolines website.
