Jobs & Career Portals

Explore global remote job boards, official government graduate visa employment pathways, and student internship finders worldwide.

🌍 Global Remote Job Boards

Platforms dedicated to finding remote positions from anywhere in the world.

  • We Work Remotely: Largest community for remote tech, marketing, and design jobs.
  • Remote.co: Curated remote listings ranging from customer support to management.
  • FlexJobs: Hand-screened professional remote and flexible employment listings.

🎓 Graduate Visa & Work Pathways

Official government resources allowing international students to transition into working careers.

  • UK Graduate Route: Allows international graduates to stay and work in the UK for at least 2 years.
  • US OPT (Optional Practical Training): F-1 visa students working in the US temporary post-graduation.
  • Germany Job Seeker / Opportunity Card: Points-based visa for skilled professionals to find work locally.

🤝 Student & Entry-Level Internships

Websites built to bridge the gap between university studies and corporate careers.

  • ErasmusIntern (Europe): Placement platform matching students with international internships in Europe.
  • Handshake: Early-career network connecting university students with global employers.
