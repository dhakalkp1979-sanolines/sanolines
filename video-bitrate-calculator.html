What is a Video Bitrate Calculator?

A Video Bitrate Calculator is an essential tool for video editors, content creators, and live streamers. It determines the optimal data rate (measured in kilobits per second or kbps) required to compress a video file to a precise target size for storage, uploading platforms, or disc media.

How to Use the Tool

  • Target File Size: Enter your desired final file size in megabytes (MB).
  • Duration: Specify the exact length of your video in minutes and seconds.
  • Audio Bitrate: Choose your preferred audio stream compression quality to subtract its footprint from the total budget.

Why Bitrate Matters for Video Quality

Bitrate dictates how much information is stored per second of video. Balancing video and audio bitrates ensures smooth playback, prevents pixelation or compression artifacts, and guarantees your media fits platform constraints.
