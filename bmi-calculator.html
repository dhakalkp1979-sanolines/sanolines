BMI Calculator

Check your body mass index and understand your health range instantly.

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a simple calculation using a person's height and weight to gauge whether they fall into a healthy weight category.

How to Use the BMI Calculator

  • Enter your current body weight in kilograms (kg).
  • Enter your height in centimeters (cm).
  • View your calculated index score and classification right away.

Understanding Your Results

Generally, a BMI under 18.5 is considered underweight, 18.5–24.9 is normal weight, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30 or higher falls into the obesity range.
