Visa & Immigration Guides

Step-by-step official government portal links for student visas, work permits, and residency requirements across the US, UK, and EU. Reliable, ad-free immigration checklists.

🇺🇸 United States (USCIS & State Department)

Official portals and processing checklists for studying, working, or temporary residency in the United States.

  • F-1 Student Visa & SEVP: Comprehensive guidance on maintaining student status, SEVIS fee payments, and initial interview checklists.
  • H-1B Specialty Occupations: Cap-subject lottery guidelines, employer sponsorship criteria, and petition filing steps via USCIS.
  • J-1 Exchange Visitor Program: Intern, trainee, and research scholar categories, including the two-year home-country physical presence requirement rules.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom (UK Visas and Immigration - GOV.UK)

Direct government pathways for student enrollment, point-based work visas, and dependent integration.

  • Student Visa (Tier 4 replacement): Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) requirements, financial proof calculators, and English language tests.
  • Skilled Worker Visa: Approved sponsor employer list, minimum salary threshold tables, and standard occupational code tracking.
  • Graduate Route Visa: Unrestricted work rights for international graduates holding a recognized UK Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD degree.

🇪🇺 European Union & Schengen Area

Navigating individual EU member state laws, the EU Blue Card system, and research mobility agreements.

  • EU Blue Card: Fast-track residency and work permits designed for highly qualified non-EU professionals across participating member states.
  • Germany Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte): Points-based job search visa allowing skilled professionals to enter Germany and look for employment locally.
  • France "Talent Passport" (Passeport Talent): Multi-year residence permit tailored for startup founders, highly skilled employees, and renowned artists.
